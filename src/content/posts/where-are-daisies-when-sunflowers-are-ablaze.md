Novelty hit the convergence. Stillness gave way to a new brew. Murmurs ran through the crowd. The usual quiet was disturbed in a flash. Sifting through the grains of sand, wafting through the land of the living, novelty dismantled the antiquated as the new norm. The past ceased its ephemeral visit, and the novel sat on its throne of the conceptual future. But as ignorance gives way to bliss, change sparks a new hope. Here comes the boiling point; get up and do something about it. What’s the ultimate truth-seeker’s final verdict on change? There never was one. And so the hum rose to a crescendo and fell as the tide of the breeze receded. The crowd mourned, chasing after it. The mystery man looked around, hooked on the feeling. When the high of life hits, it shatters your cosmetic chains. What’s purity if not a vessel waiting patiently for a granule of imperfection? The talkers are loudest before the great shunning. A thing said here, another there. Until it’s gone. The gathering dies down, with certainty again, and even surer hope. A certain desire for locomotion, for overcoming the mysteries of life. But never to understand them, since that’s a fool’s errand.

“Who are we?”

What remains, if not this uneasy silence? Death is a stalker, and it doesn’t believe in quick kills. It seeks out its prey, simmers it among the seams of its broth of unbeknownst suffering, and puts the heat on low.

Green days of glee passed by as the singers nipped their lips shut. These lives of litter were meant not for thee. The deaths of our children were sent by the shepherds of the true spirit, singing among their children. The long before, to come forward. The long end, to last forever. The singers, long forgotten, their reality now conspicuous. What once was a jovial crowd had always been a patch of dry grassland.

*Belittling are thoughts*  
*When comparison erupts*  
*Do remember their words*  
*It’s the other’s thinking*  
*Which is naught*

Stasis isn’t meant for the soul; a bereaved mother must always learn to live within a desert. A desert, not sought after, but formulated through considerable depth of character. This desert of sorrowless joy, of emptiness far beyond mental endurance, is where peace meets apathy. The brain, seeking absolute righteousness, prevails in all forms, optimising incongruity. Which reality must the mother look after, then? One without a child, or the one without childlessness? Alas, there is an argument within the argument. Stasis is for the good of the soul; death is a fact only as long as one lives. What runs through our hero’s mind? He is shattered, thinking of locomotion. The words change; the emotions stay. Words are quibbling with themselves, life singing for itself. What comes with thought? A choice already made. What comes with tomorrow? Today.

*Pearlescent days of glee*  
*Come for thee*  
*Must you stay?*  
*Darkness answers some days*  
*A joy forever bleak on others*  
*Are you truly free when there’s*  
*Always a need for you to be?*

You break the walls around you to become one without peace. Whatever can be demolished must be demolished. This epidemic of loneliness is still not so crucial, in the end. You lose your inhibitions, and you lose your sense of rites. Whatever stays is a lingering need for unchanging growth. Ever-present familiarity shall melt your heart, in the end.

Wafting back to reality, we have our own sonar of truth. A small dollop of hope. Our one and only, ignominious man. Naked from head to toe, walking past the grass, into the land of mirrors. His mind reeled with untold knowledge, all the facts unknown. Fractured by the unsung vows of this indelible life, a little focus goes a long way. To not observe is futile, and to not think, impossible. The concrete slabs were huge and tall, his reflexive aura small and brittle. However much you walk, son, you must balk in front of your new Gods. A wish here, a claim there, resulting in severance everywhere. Which parts of life did he still have time to focus on? Whenever life called, he answered the wrong line. He couldn’t let his mistakes die down. What was required? It was the unrequirement; the belated wishes of righteousness were calling. Calling to let it die down. Let the cradle die. What is the greatest moral act one must perform? Must you revolt against anguish, or against revolt? Angst for life comes calling. Let it die. Let your emotions die. For they will come back, hosting new realities for life, just to feel the pain of losing. Just to shatter the cogs of contentment. To understand, first and foremost, that it ends with the cycle, not the beginning. Why rush away from the cities, then? The metropolitan is a marvel for a few, doom for all. Then why, I ask, do you wish to run towards the daisies in the grass? Where is your solitude when it comes inching towards the things you hold precious? Where are daisies when sunflowers are ablaze?

And thus the walk began, fraught with granules of empiricism. Wherever you look, you see. Then you think, and then you feel. Some say the null vectors of space were what he was after; most say they haven’t studied mathematics. There have always been two of you, and there always must be one of you. When clarity hits the dome, do not forget your guard, for you are about to make the largest of errors. You are about to fulfil the strongest of convictions. And yet, you are not at fault here.

“Now you walk, but you must run. Now you walk, but you must run. Now you walk, but you must run. Now you walk…”

The ringing didn’t stop. Aden kept walking until his feet felt numb and his legs screamed with agonising crucifixion. As willpower balked in front of necessity, he drew close to a fenced-off alleyway. The door was left open; inside, a dog was barking out its prayers.

*Forever I must seek*  
*Forever I am reek*  
*The greatest dungeonmaster*  
*The owner of no keys*  
*Singing among other things*  
*La belle vie*

As writers of art, it is our duty to be just. To value our perceived facts and devalue those which aren’t. Whenever that thought has come to me during the writing of this excerpt, I have beseeched my mind for needs of pretension, safety, whatever you might wanna call it. Truth be told, I’ll say it out loud. I have been martyred to narrative incoherence.
