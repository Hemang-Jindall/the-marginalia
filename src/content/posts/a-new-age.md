description: "A poem about change, contradiction, obedience, and the search for solace."
pubDate: 2025-08-30

bequeathing this insanity

oh some days great

and some days without levity

a new age comes before me

astounding belligerence

truculent obedience is all but glee

rocking myself to sleep

holding the arms of this heaven

that i forever seek
