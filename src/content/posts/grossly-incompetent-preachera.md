who’s your leader pastor?

the divinity of the spirit

or the seams of your sentience?

what is, per say, datum?

preach to me the science of love!

it’s a collective error, however

the death of my own despair

beyond the scopes of my life

and as a perveyor of holy strife

take your claims, forgo

let go, oh selfish being

don’t make me weep

my heart is jocund
