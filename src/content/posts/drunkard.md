---
title: "Drunkard"
description: "A brief encounter with an old man whose words circle around death."
pubDate: 2025-06-14
category: "Poetry"
draft: false
---

the old dainty fool was unbothered

standing solemn meagerly he muttered

what I heard is what he said

mumbling and groveling about death

so close to my face his whiskey sputtered
