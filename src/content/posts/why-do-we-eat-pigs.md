I am a buffoon, a hypocrite. About fifteen days ago, I was reading Animal Farm. With all the pigs running things, my mind wasn't capable of acknowledging the direct connection between the intelligence of pigs in the book, and in real world. Why do we eat pigs? No, seriously. We eat them because they are tasty, nutritious, and in some areas of the world, maybe a necessity when it comes to food. BUT. Let's look beyond that for a split second.

Why are there millions of pigs in pens living, not in solitude, but in dirt, grit, rolled over on their bellies, not wanting to get up, unwilling to live, even. Because of humans. We have tainted the earth forever. It's funny, really, I jump from learning in religion about the various epiphanies of human beings around the world, them realizing how God loves them, how God is real. I hope, for the sake of all humanity, there isn't a God. I pray to God, please end your existence.

For when time comes to answer for what we have caused on this earth, no one is walking through the pearly gates, not one single soul. Rest in peace, all the animals that knew, up until the last breath they took, they were going to die and felt agonizing pain. Rest in peace.

## June 15, 2025

Hah, it’s funny. I am reading Animal Liberation presently and it’s just so much worse. Most pigs don’t even have even room to move their necks around. This is sickening. There are children. Children pigs who are fed liquids, bred for 1.5 years in a cage where they cannot even move an inch and sent for slaughter. If anyone can’t see the issue with this, the issue is within them.
