Ambition is the driving force behind entrepreneurship. But what happens when the root cause of that ambition is unethical?

As individuals, we are prone to weakness. These fragilities make you whole, curating a sense of longing for betterment. Difficult moments shape the very core of our beings. In the modern era, hardship is ineffective, barren. And yet, the mind tends to run on overdrive, producing a redolent awe for sanctity. Sanctity, which once formulated in the mind, leads to the pursuit of wealth.

The entrepreneur is looking for change, looking to lead. But as once a great man said, “the measure of a man is what he does with power,” not all humans are equally gifted when it comes to morality. An entrepreneur may create all he wants, but his ambition is driven by emotivism. What one wants, one strives for. As self-conscious beings, selfishness is a major part of our genetic makeup, shaping our moral identity and deriving motives. Selfish motives overcome avowed goodwill, and the man becomes a shell.

Take a moment to reflect, in this journey of yours, about the rat driving your actions.
