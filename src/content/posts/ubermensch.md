Are you a follower or a leader? We deal with these questions awfully literally, leaving no time for contemplation. A figurative leader is one to be showered with accolades, fawned over. A billion layers, your thinking goes, prying through your defenses, etching unwritten conclusions. This is a legend, and that, a wasted living.

How often are our beliefs challenged? How often are our morals questioned? A follower is an optimist. A leader, a petty juvenile. A little difference here. A big shattering there. Walk a little more, next time maybe push a little more. Make yourself hurt, have yourself be questioned. May not learn, yet persevere.

Friedrich Nietzsche has talked about Übermensch, a being who doesn’t follow, rejects society, observes linearity in thinking, and stands for his own morals. Kill dogs, eat rocks. A crazy idea is what you are. That dream is what they don’t want you to achieve. The entrepreneur is not the one who obeyed rules, he created his own. Make your own stand, the great ones already have.

Pick yourself up. The world isn’t bare, your mind is. Death isn’t a door to be walked towards, life is not an experience to be left hollow. What kind of person is capable of rejecting societal norms and inherited values?
