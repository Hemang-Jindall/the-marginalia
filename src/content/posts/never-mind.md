title: "Never Mind"
description: "A poem about anxiety, mortality, missed connection, and the aftermath of an ordinary encounter."
pubDate: 2025-09-29

Leaning haptics soak your time

ever moving forever mine

Oh you believe you’re free is it?

must not try, I must not flee

it is tonight that we see a forever sun

a darkened glee

it grows, it comes for you

it saves face, running after you

nothing can prepare the dangers from death

oh no that’s exactly what i must check

it’s such a pain just in grief

when I walk in the corridor

There finally comes a familiar face

never mind

I ruined it yesterday.
