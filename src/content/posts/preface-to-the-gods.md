here, lord take some from me

and another, yet another

keep them coming because I am justice

I am the omen of truth and I forgive

I forgive you lord, for my bestowed options

These options of which you speak of so little

Coming days there is another choice of life

Of death, of desire, of pain, of pleasure, of you

But I must say, oh great seeker, let me love myself for I

for I for I for I can't; but I must I must and I truthfully must
What is it that you desire? What is it that you want me to desire?

Here's another offering, the offering of my life for the cost of another's

What is the ultimate moral act?
