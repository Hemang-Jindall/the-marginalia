Just finished reading Animal Liberation by Peter Singer. It was a good read, albeit the last two chapters I could do without. But it really goes to prove Peter Singer’s earlier points. If this book doesn’t convince a person about liberating animals, I don’t know what will. But other than that, I think Peter Singer should have added a seventh chapter discussing whether this will work or not.

Look, I understand that speciesism is wrong, I understand that I must not eat meat, I understand that I must not eat animal products, I understand that we have a moral duty to our conscience to follow through on what we believe in. I am a practicing vegan today. But a voice remains. A nagging, annoying voice that keeps shouting, “It probably won’t work.”

Yeah, I understand that more and more people are becoming vegan, but what we usually don’t factor in is that even more and more people are becoming carnistic. With all the environmental damage and political scuffles in the world today, will this future ever come to be? Will humans ever stop eating animals? Science shows that it is harmful to our health, but eating animals and their products has become second nature to us. For 2,000 years. For forever. The current growth rates of veganism aren’t sufficient.

Is there a future where we don’t eat animals and their products? This is such a fallacy because, in my heart of hearts, I want to believe there is. I want to believe that humanity learns. It changes. It grows. And so there will be a point in our lifetime where the one hindrance to affluent health will be our consumption of animals. Maybe artificial intelligence will dictate which animals are to be spared now. Maybe all will be spared. Maybe.

But. But. But.

It won’t happen. No, it won’t. A man today eating a burger thinks, that’s a bit too unhealthy for me, I’ll change my lifestyle. The second day it repeats. The third day it repeats. The change of lifestyle doesn’t occur. The man dies eating burgers. There is no sanctity in the suffering of animals when I am hungry for some delicious food. I hate what has happened in this world.

The man eats, the factory feeds.

It is probably pills that will fix our needs.
